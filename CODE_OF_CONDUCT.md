# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with empathy.
- Protect private information and avoid sharing another person's content
  without permission.
- Prioritize the health and safety of the community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. If harmful conduct
occurs in a GitHub issue, pull request, or comment, use GitHub's content
reporting and blocking tools where appropriate; maintainers may also act on
conduct they can observe in project spaces.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project or its community.
